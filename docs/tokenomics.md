# Tokenomics

Status: no numbers announced. This document records what the product intends and what is deliberately left empty.

## Shape

Hidon is a launchpad. Each AI personality created on it gets a coin of its own, on Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663). The coin is how the character's economy works: backers hold it, and holding it ties them to the persona's future.

There is one coin per persona. Whether the platform itself has a token has not been announced.

## Known

- Chain: Robinhood Chain, chain id 4663.
- Contract address: none published.
- Coin per persona: yes, that is the product.

## Principles

1. **Publish the address first.** The contract slot at the top of the site opens only when an address is stated. Until then, treat any address seen elsewhere as unconfirmed.
2. **No hidden mint.** Supply rules, and who may change them, are written down in full before launch.
3. **Locks are public.** Anything locked is locked onchain with a schedule anyone can read.
4. **Fees are stated plainly.** If a coin carries a fee, the amount, the recipient and the way it can change appear in one place.
5. **A coin is a stake, not a forecast.** Personas never talk about price or tell anyone to buy.
6. **Creators and communities both count.** The split between a persona's creator, its community and the platform is a design question to settle in public, not in a footnote.

## What would change this page

A published, verifiable contract address and a written specification. Until both exist, this file stays a statement of what is open.
